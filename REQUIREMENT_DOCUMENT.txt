--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
                         SINGLE-LINE REQUIREMENT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

"We require a desktop-based POS Cash Counter application for Zada Pharmacy
that records real-time Cash and Online payment transactions, manages shift
openings with petty cash floats, performs physical cash denomination
reconciliation at shift closing, detects shortages or surpluses, and
generates printable audit slips — all stored locally in a secure SQLite
database for permanent record keeping."

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
                         SCOPE OF DELIVERY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. Real-time transaction entry (Cash & Online) with invoice tracking
2. Shift opening with configurable petty cash float
3. Physical cash denomination counter (Rs 5000 / 1000 / 500 / 100 / 50 / 20 / 10 / Coins)
4. Automatic shortage/surplus variance detection
5. Printable thermal/A4 audit closing slips
6. Complete ledger history with search, filter, and export (CSV/Print)
7. All closings archive with summary reports
